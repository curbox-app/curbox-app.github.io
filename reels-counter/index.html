Reels counter: see exactly how many you watch.

Curbox puts a live reels counter on your screen while you scroll, so you can count how many Reels and Shorts you watch as it happens. One number covers Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels, and Curbox also tracks your attention span: how long you actually stay on each video.

Why count Reels instead of minutes?

Minutes are slippery in a short-video feed. Each clip is only a few seconds, so the time never feels like it adds up, and "20 minutes" is easy to wave away. A count is harder to ignore. "You have watched 80 videos today" lands differently, because you can picture eighty separate things you will not remember.

Instagram and YouTube do not show you this number. Their own time tools treat the whole app as one block, so an hour of messaging friends and an hour of Reels look the same. The Curbox counter only counts the short-video player itself.

How the reels counter decides what counts

The counter works through Curbox's accessibility service, which can tell when a short-video player is on screen. Here is what happens to a single video.

Is it a short-form video in a supported app?

Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels are supported. Community contributors have also added support for some modded clients, which the project lists but does not endorse.

Did you actually stop on it?

Reels you skip past in a fast swipe are not counted, so flicking through ten clips to find one does not inflate your number. The count reflects videos you stayed on.

The count is combined across every supported app. Ten Instagram Reels and ten YouTube Shorts make twenty. Switching apps does not reset anything, which matters because that is exactly what people do when one feed gets boring.

An attention span tracker for short videos

The count tells you how much. Attention span tells you how. If your average time per video keeps dropping, you are skimming faster and faster for the next hit, which is a useful thing to notice even if the total count stays the same.

Turn on the counter

  1. Install Curbox from the downloads page and finish onboarding, which grants the accessibility service the counter relies on.
  2. Go to Reducers → Short-Form Video Counter and switch it on.
  3. Adjust the text size, text opacity and background color so the number is visible without covering the video. A small, semi-transparent number in a corner is easy to live with.
  4. Open Reels or Shorts and watch the number go up as you scroll.

The same screen shows TOTAL TODAY and a weekly bar chart; use the arrows to look at earlier weeks. For a count you see without opening any app, add the Reels Widget from Usage tab → ⋮ → Pin Widget. More detail is in the Short-Form Video Counter docs.

Reels counter FAQ

How do I count how many Reels I watch?

Instagram does not show this. Curbox adds a live counter on screen: go to Reducers, open Short-Form Video Counter and switch it on. The number goes up as you watch, and a weekly chart keeps the history.

Does it count YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels too?

Yes. Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels all go into one combined count, so switching apps does not reset it.

Do skipped Reels count?

No. Reels you skip past in a fast swipe are not counted, so the number reflects the videos you actually stayed on.

What is attention span tracking?

Curbox measures how long you actually watch each short video and shows your average attention span in your usage stats, so you can see whether you are skimming faster over time.

Can the counter block Reels after a number?

The counter only counts. To stop at a number, turn on Short-Form Video Content in Reducers and choose Reel Count Based. It uses the same combined count across apps.
