Account · Data deletion

Delete My Account

Enter your email and password to submit a deletion request. We'll verify your identity and remove your account and all associated data.

Submit a deletion request

Once verified, your account and sync data will be permanently deleted. This cannot be undone.

If you entered your correct email and password, we'll delete your account in 5 working days.

You'll receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete. If you don't hear from us within 5 working days, please contact us at support@curbox.app.

What gets deleted

  • Your account credentials and profile
  • All synced usage data and settings
  • Any stored session or focus history tied to your account

Data stored locally on your device is not affected. You can clear it from within the app at any time.
