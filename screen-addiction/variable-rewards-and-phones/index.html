Variable rewards: the slot machine effect in your phone

Variable rewards are rewards that come at unpredictable times. They are one of the main reasons that feeds, notifications and likes are so hard to put down. The same principle makes slot machines so compelling.

What are variable rewards?

A variable reward is a reward that you cannot predict. Sometimes an action brings a reward, sometimes it brings nothing, and you do not know which result comes next. Habits built on variable rewards are usually stronger and harder to stop than habits built on fixed rewards.

The idea comes from behavioral psychology. In experiments on operant conditioning, some animals got food on an unpredictable schedule. They kept pressing a lever for longer than animals that got food every time. Psychologists call this a variable ratio schedule. It is the same principle that a slot machine uses.

Why the slot machine effect is so strong

With a fixed reward, you know what to expect. If a vending machine always gives you a snack, you use it when you are hungry and then you stop. With a variable reward, every try could be the big one. So you try again.

  • Hope keeps the habit going. After an empty try, the next try still feels possible. There is never a clear moment to give up.
  • The anticipation matters. Research suggests that the brain reacts strongly to the expectation of a reward, not only to the reward itself. The dopamine page explains this in plain terms.
  • Each try is fast and cheap. A swipe or a refresh takes less than a second. A low cost per try means many tries.
  • Near misses count. An almost good post or an almost interesting message can still pull you forward.

Where variable rewards hide in your phone

The feed

Every swipe in a feed is a small spin. One post is boring, the next is funny, the third is shocking. You keep swiping because the next post might be the good one. Short-form video makes each spin even faster. See reels and shorts addiction.

Pull to refresh

The pull-down gesture works much like a lever. You pull, you wait a moment, and the result is new content or the same content. Many people refresh again and again with no reason.

Likes and comments

When you post something, you do not know how many likes it will get. That uncertainty makes you check the post many times. Each check is another try.

Notifications and messages

A buzz could be a friend, a bill or an ad. Because you cannot know, every buzz pulls you to the screen. See notification addiction.

Games and loot boxes

Loot boxes, random card packs and prize wheels in games are the most direct form of variable rewards. Some countries regulate loot boxes because of their similarity to gambling. See mobile game addiction.

How to weaken the variable reward loop

You cannot remove the uncertainty from an app. You can make each try less frequent, less rewarding and more deliberate.

  1. Reduce the number of tries. Check messages and feeds at fixed times, not whenever you feel the pull.
  2. Make each try cost more. Add a short delay before an app opens. A few seconds of waiting breaks the fast loop.
  3. Set the stop point in advance. Decide how many videos or minutes you allow before you open the app.
  4. Hide the numbers. Some apps let you hide like counts. Turn off notifications for likes and comments.
  5. Make the reward flatter. Grayscale removes bright colors that make each post feel exciting.
  6. Get a fixed reward instead. A book chapter, a walk or a call gives a reliable reward. Reliable rewards do not hook you in the same way.

Questions

What are variable rewards?

Variable rewards are rewards that come at unpredictable times. You do not know if the next action brings a reward or nothing. Habits built on variable rewards are usually stronger and harder to stop than habits with fixed rewards.

Why are phones compared to slot machines?

Feeds, pull to refresh, likes and notifications all give unpredictable rewards. Each swipe or check is like a spin: sometimes good, often nothing. Slot machines use the same principle, a variable ratio schedule.

What is a variable ratio schedule?

A variable ratio schedule is a pattern from behavioral psychology where a reward comes after an unpredictable number of actions. It tends to produce very persistent behavior, because the next action always might bring the reward.

How do I stop the slot machine effect on my phone?

Check apps at fixed times, add a short delay before apps open, and set a video or time limit before you start. Turn off like notifications, and replace the scroll with an activity that gives a reliable reward.

Are loot boxes variable rewards?

Yes. Loot boxes, random card packs and prize wheels give random prizes, which is a direct form of variable rewards. Some countries regulate loot boxes because they are similar to gambling.
