Screen addiction · Phone addiction

Screen addiction: what it is, why it happens and how to stop it.

Screen addiction is screen use that you find hard to control and that starts to hurt your sleep, work, mood or relationships. These guides explain the signs and causes in plain words. Then they show practical steps to take back control of your phone.

Start here

Phone addiction is not a weakness. Many apps use design that makes it hard to stop, such as feeds with no end and rewards that come at random times. A plan that changes your environment works better than a plan that needs willpower every time.

New to the topic? Read what screen addiction is, take the phone addiction test, then follow how to break phone addiction.

This information is general and is not medical advice. If phone use causes serious problems in your life, talk to a doctor or a mental health professional.

Questions

What is screen addiction?

Screen addiction is a pattern of screen use that you find hard to control and that causes problems in your life. It can affect sleep, work, school, mood or relationships. It is not a formal diagnosis in the DSM-5, but the problems it causes are real.

How do I know if I am addicted to my phone?

Look at what happens, not only at the hours. Warning signs include using the phone longer than you planned, failed attempts to cut back, feeling restless without it, and lost sleep or work. Our phone addiction test gives you a quick self-check.

How can I stop screen addiction?

Change your environment instead of relying on willpower. Measure your real use, add a pause before the apps that pull you in, set limits in a calm moment, and plan something to do instead. An app blocker like Curbox can do the first three for you.
