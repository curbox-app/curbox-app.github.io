Feature · Auto DND

Schedule Do Not Disturb automatically.

Want your Android phone to go quiet every night, every work block, or whenever you sit down to focus, without remembering to swipe a toggle? Here is how to set an auto Do Not Disturb schedule with what Android already has, and where Curbox's Auto DND earns its place.

Option 1: Android's built-in Do Not Disturb schedule

Start here. Almost every Android phone can schedule Do Not Disturb with no extra app, and for a fixed bedtime it is often all you need. The menus move around between phone makers and Android versions, so treat these as directions rather than exact paths:

Recent Pixel phonesSettings → Modes. Add a mode or edit Bedtime, then set a time schedule. Older versions call this Do Not Disturb → Schedules.
Older stock AndroidSettings → Sound (or Sound & vibration) → Do Not Disturb → Schedules.
Samsung and othersLook under Settings → Notifications → Do not disturb, or search Settings for "Do not disturb" and look for a schedule option.

In the same place you decide what still gets through: calls from favourites, repeat callers, alarms, and specific apps. Curbox does not change those rules. When it switches Do Not Disturb on, your phone's exceptions still apply.

If all you want is "silent from 11 PM to 7 AM", use the built-in schedule and skip the rest of this page. Curbox adds value when quiet time should follow your focus, not just the clock.

Option 2: Curbox Auto DND

Auto DND is one of Curbox's Mindful Nudges. It switches Do Not Disturb on automatically on the schedule you set, and it sits in the same app as your app limits and focus sessions, so your quiet hours and your blocked hours live in one place.

  1. Open Reducers → Auto DND.
  2. Tap + and name the group, for example "Nights".
  3. Select the time schedule you want.
  4. Tap Done. Curbox asks for Do Not Disturb access the first time.

The midnight rule. One time range in Curbox cannot cross midnight. For 11 PM to 7 AM, add two ranges: 11 PM to 12 AM and 12 AM to 7 AM. That is why the night blocks in the week chart above are drawn in two pieces. Full details are in the Auto DND docs.

Turn on Do Not Disturb automatically when you focus

Fixed schedules miss the moments you decide on the spot: "I'm going to study for the next hour." For those, Curbox's Focus Mode has a Turn on do not disturb option in each Focus Group. Start a session and your phone goes quiet for exactly that long, alongside blocking the apps you chose. When the timer ends, both lift together.

You can start that session by tapping a programmed NFC tag on your desk, or let another app start it through the Curbox API, which can also request a Do Not Disturb change directly. Pair it with a Pomodoro cycle for structured work blocks.

Which one should you use?

Is your quiet time the same every day?
YesUse Android's built-in schedule. It is already on your phone and needs no extra permissions.
No, it depends on the dayUse focus sessions with Do Not Disturb, started when you begin work.
Should quiet time also block distracting apps?
YesUse a Curbox Focus Group with Do Not Disturb on. One action silences and blocks.
NoA plain Do Not Disturb schedule, built-in or Auto DND, is enough.
Is full silence too much?
YesTry Scheduled Notifications: messages arrive in batches at set times instead of not at all.
NoKeep Do Not Disturb, and check your exceptions so alarms and important callers still reach you.

The softer option: batch instead of silence

Do Not Disturb is all or nothing. Scheduled Notifications, another Curbox reducer, holds notifications as they arrive and hands them back together at the times you pick, 9 AM, 1 PM and 6 PM by default and up to 12 times a day. Calls, alarms, ongoing notifications like music or maps, and any app you allow still come through right away. It needs notification access, and Curbox does not read, save or send notification content anywhere.

Many people use both: Auto DND overnight, batched notifications during the day.

Auto Do Not Disturb FAQ

How do I make Do Not Disturb turn on automatically on Android?

Open Settings and find Do Not Disturb or Modes (often under Sound or Notifications), then add a schedule with the days and hours you want. Names and menus differ between phone makers and Android versions. Curbox can also do it: Reducers, then Auto DND, then add a group with a time schedule.

Do I need an app to schedule Do Not Disturb?

Usually not. Most Android phones can schedule Do Not Disturb on their own. An app such as Curbox is worth it when you want silence tied to focus sessions, set up next to your app limits, or started by an NFC tag.

Why does my overnight Auto DND schedule in Curbox not work?

A single Curbox time range cannot cross midnight. For 11 PM to 7 AM, create two ranges: 11 PM to 12 AM and 12 AM to 7 AM. The same rule applies to App Pause and short-form video schedules.

Will alarms and calls still come through?

That depends on your phone's Do Not Disturb exceptions, which you set in Android's own settings. Curbox switches Do Not Disturb on and off; what is allowed through while it is on follows your phone's rules.

What permission does Auto DND need?

Do Not Disturb access (notification policy access). Curbox asks for it only when you turn on Auto DND or the Focus Mode Do Not Disturb option.

Is there something gentler than Do Not Disturb?

Yes. Scheduled Notifications holds notifications and delivers them together at times you choose, up to 12 a day, while calls, alarms and apps you allow still come through right away.
