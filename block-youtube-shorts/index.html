YouTube, Shorts.

YouTube is useful. Shorts are the part that eats an evening. The YouTube app has no setting to turn them off, but on Android you can block Shorts and keep everything you actually open YouTube for.

Blocked

  • The Shorts player, after your limit
  • youtube.com/shorts in the browser
  • The endless Shorts swipe
  • The recommendation feed F-Droid

Still works

  • Regular videos and playlists
  • Search
  • Subscriptions and channels
  • Tutorials, lectures, music

Three methods, ranked

All three use Curbox, a free and open-source Android app blocker with no ads and no internet permission. They stack, so you can use one, two or all of them. Ranked by how much of the problem each one solves for a typical person.

1Start here

Put a limit on Shorts inside the YouTube app

The Short-Form Video Content reducer watches for the Shorts player. Once you hit a daily limit, Shorts are blocked with a pause screen, and you are back on regular YouTube. You choose the limit as time (minutes of Shorts per day) or as a count (number of Shorts). Shorts you swipe past quickly are not counted.

Coverage Setup Bypass effort
Reducers → Short-Form Video Content → main switch on → Time Based or Reel Count Based → set limit → Configure Warning Screen

The limit is shared with Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels, so moving from Shorts to Reels does not reset anything: 10 Shorts plus 10 Reels is 20 against a limit of 20. To make Shorts effectively disappear, set a tiny limit and choose Never Unlock on the warning screen. To keep a little, use a wait or a task instead. Full options are in the Short-Form Video docs.

If you use a modded YouTube client, the community has added support for YouTube ReVanced and Morphe. That is a community contribution: the Curbox project does not endorse mods.

2Close the gap

Block Shorts pages in the browser

Blocking Shorts in the app sends a lot of people to m.youtube.com. The Keywords And Websites reducer reads the browser address bar and shows the pause screen on matching pages. Because Shorts live under their own path, you can block them without touching the rest of the site.

Coverage Setup Bypass effort
Reducers → Keywords And Websites → + → add youtube.com/shorts → Done

Use youtube.com/shorts for YouTube only, or /shorts to block that path on every site at once. Avoid pasting a single Shorts link: that only blocks one video. This works in Chrome, Brave, Firefox, Opera and several privacy browsers; see blocking websites on Android for the full list and a pattern tester.

3Go further

Hide the feed so Shorts never get offered F-Droid / GitHub build

Methods 1 and 2 stop Shorts once you are in them. UI Hider stops YouTube from showing you the bait. It covers parts of the YouTube screen while the app stays usable, with two ready-made rules:

  • Hide the feed so only search results are visible. You open YouTube, search for what you came for, and leave.
  • Hide everything except the video, which removes recommendations, comments and the description on the watch page.
Coverage Setup Bypass effort
Reducers → UI Hider → Enable UI Hider → switch on the YouTube rules

UI Hider is in the F-Droid and GitHub builds of Curbox, not the Playstore build. It is marked experimental: YouTube changes its layout from time to time, and a rule can stop working until it is updated. If you are comfortable with code, UI Hider has its own sandboxed scripting language for writing custom rules. See the UI Hider docs.

Make the pause screen count

Whatever you block, the pause screen decides whether the block holds. A button you can tap instantly is a speed bump. Some settings that work well for Shorts specifically:

  • Delay timer: a short countdown, during which the phone vibrates, before any option appears. Ten seconds is often enough to notice you are on autopilot.
  • State your intent: type why you want more Shorts. Your reasons are saved, and reading them back later is humbling.
  • Proceed limits: allow getting through a couple of times, then not again until the window resets.
  • Urge Surfing: an "I'm struggling" button that walks you through a three-minute break instead of unlocking.

All of these are in the unlock challenges guide.

Questions

Can I turn off YouTube Shorts in YouTube settings?
No. The YouTube app has no setting that turns Shorts off. To stop them you need an outside tool such as Curbox, which can block the Shorts player while normal videos keep working.
Will blocking Shorts block normal YouTube videos?
No. Curbox's Short-Form Video reducer only reacts to the Shorts player. Regular videos, search, subscriptions and playlists still work. In a browser, the keyword youtube.com/shorts blocks only Shorts pages.
How do I block YouTube Shorts in Chrome on Android?
Add youtube.com/shorts, or /shorts to cover every site, as a keyword in Curbox's Keywords And Websites reducer. It works in Chrome, Brave, Firefox and other supported browsers.
Does Curbox work with YouTube ReVanced?
The community has added Shorts support for YouTube ReVanced and some other modded clients. The Curbox project does not endorse mods.
Is there a way to block Shorts on a computer too?
Yes. The Curbox browser extension for Chrome and Firefox blocks websites and parts of websites with schedules and limits.
