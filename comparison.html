Why Curbox wins on Android

Keep the useful part

Block Reels or Shorts without losing messages, search, subscriptions, or the rest of the app.

Friction that fits you

Choose a complete lock, a wait, adaptive math, written intent, NFC, QR/barcode scanning, or another challenge.

Understand the scroll

See not only how many short videos you watched, but also how that scrolling affects your attention span.

No premium wall

Curbox is free and open source. Its strongest blocking and unlock tools are available without a subscription.

No blocker is best for every person. Reef is a clean choice for classic blocking and focus timers. DetoxDroid is excellent for grayscale and interrupting doomscrolling. Curbox stands out by putting feed controls, app-part hiding, flexible unlock challenges, and short-video attention insights together in one free Android app.
