A YourHour alternative for the step after knowing.

YourHour is good at one thing a lot of people need first: showing you, plainly, how much you use your phone. Unlock counts, an addiction level, a floating timer on screen. When people search for a YourHour alternative, it is usually because knowing stopped being enough. Here is where each app sits on the climb from knowing to stopping, and how to take the next step in Curbox.

What YourHour does

YourHour: Screen Time Control is made by Mindefy Technologies, based in Indore, India, and runs on Android and iPhone. Its product page lists screen time monitoring by day and week across apps and categories, usage analysis, customisable reminders and alerts, curated and scheduled challenges, daily, weekly and monthly reports with a daily push report, a "Mindful Pause" reminder when you go over a limit you set, and usage pattern graphs that sort you into one of four behavioural types.

Its privacy policy adds the details that make it distinctive: it tracks screen time, app launches and unlock counts, gives you an addiction level, can show a floating timer over other apps, and uses the accessibility service to show a timer or overlay when you open an app you have marked. It also says your usage is stored on your device and that it does not upload your specific app usage patterns to its servers. That is a good privacy stance, and worth crediting.

When YourHour is the better pick

  • Unlock counts and app launches matter to you. Curbox does not track either.
  • You are motivated by levels and challenges. YourHour's addiction scale and scheduled challenges have no Curbox equivalent.
  • You use an iPhone. YourHour has an iOS app; Curbox does not yet.
  • You only want awareness. If a timer in the corner is all it takes, you do not need a blocker.

The trade on the other side: Mindefy's privacy policy names Google AdMob for ads and Firebase for crash reports. Curbox has no ad code, no analytics and no internet permission, and its source is public under GPL v3.

YourHour alternative FAQ

What is the best YourHour alternative for Android?

It depends on why you are leaving. If you want the same tracking without ads, Curbox is free and open source with no ad code. If you want reminders that turn into real limits, Curbox can block an app at a daily cap or outside set hours and ask for effort before you continue. If you mainly like YourHour's addiction levels and challenges, Curbox has no equivalent.

Does YourHour show ads?

Mindefy's privacy policy lists Google AdMob "for ads" among the services it uses, and says AdMob may collect a device advertising identifier. We could not confirm from the Play listing how ads appear in the app today, so check the listing yourself.

Does YourHour upload my usage?

Mindefy's privacy policy says usage data is stored on your device and that it does not upload your specific app usage patterns to its servers. It does use Firebase for crash reports and AdMob for ads. Curbox goes one step further: the Android app has no internet permission at all.

Does Curbox have a floating timer like YourHour?

Curbox can show a live session timer for how long you have been in the current app, and Mindful Messages can overlay text with {live_session_duration} or {app_usage_today} on any app you choose. There is no unlock counter.

Does Curbox have addiction levels or challenges?

No. Curbox shows your numbers without labels or scores. The closest things are Focus Stats, which track your focus day streak, and Urge Surfing, which records how much your cravings drop.

Can I export my data from Curbox?

Yes. In the Usage tab, the three-dot menu has Export, which saves a Daily Breakdown, a Total Summary or both as CSV.
