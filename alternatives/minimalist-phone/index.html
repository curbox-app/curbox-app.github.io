A Minimalist Phone alternative, and why a launcher is only half of it.

minimalist phone replaces your home screen with a calm, text-only list, and it has grown an app blocker, schedules, and a grayscale mode along the way. If you are looking for a Minimalist Phone alternative, the useful question is which job you want done. A launcher changes what you see when you unlock. A blocker changes what happens when you open something anyway. Curbox is a blocker, and it works well next to a free minimal launcher.

01The home screen is the launcher's job

This is what minimalist phone is best at: an icon-free home screen, custom themes and fonts, folders, renamed apps, and hidden apps. Curbox is not a launcher and does not touch your home screen. If seeing fewer bright icons is what helps you, you want a launcher, whether minimalist phone or another one.

02The open is where both act

minimalist phone can add an optional mindful delay before an app opens, block apps for set periods, and run blocking schedules. Curbox puts a pause screen in front of an app and lets you choose what it takes to get through: nothing at all, a wait, a vibrating countdown, a QR or barcode scan, an NFC tag, adaptive math, typing a sentence, or stating your intent.

03Inside the app is where a blocker earns its keep

A launcher cannot see what happens once an app is open. minimalist phone's Play listing does now mention blocking short videos on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and Snapchat, plus in-app reminders. Curbox goes further here: it limits Reels and Shorts by time or by count, combined across apps, shows a live counter, and in the F-Droid variant its UI Hider can hide the Instagram feed or YouTube recommendations while the rest of the app works.

04Across the day, they overlap

minimalist phone has a notification filter and blocking schedules. Curbox has Scheduled Notifications, which hold notifications and deliver them together at times you pick, plus Auto DND, Focus Mode with Pomodoro, and full usage stats with CSV export.

If you like the launcher idea but want the blocking to be free and open, split the work:

  1. Pick a launcher. Keep minimalist phone just for the home screen, or try a free one. Olauncher, for example, is a GPL v3 minimal launcher on F-Droid. It is a launcher only, with no blocking, and its F-Droid listing shows full network access alongside a daily new wallpaper feature.
  2. Leave only tools on the home screen. Distracting apps stay installed but reachable only through search, which already adds a small step.
  3. Let Curbox guard the open. Reducers → Apps → +, pick the apps you hid, choose Usage Based or Time Based, and set a warning screen with a wait or an effort challenge.
  4. Handle the inside. Turn on Short-Form Video Content limits, and in the F-Droid variant the UI Hider rules for Instagram and YouTube.
  5. Avoid doubling up. If you keep minimalist phone's blocker on as well, give each tool different apps so their pause screens do not stack.

When minimalist phone is the better pick

Stay with it if the home screen is the point. A calm, icon-free launcher with folders, fonts, and themes is something Curbox does not try to be. It is also on iPhone, supports work profile apps, and bundles launcher and blocker in one place, which some people prefer to running two apps.

Switch the blocking part to Curbox if you want it free with no trial clock, if you would rather your app activity never leave the phone, or if you need stronger unlock friction, count-based Reels limits, or feed hiding.

Minimalist Phone alternative FAQ

Is Curbox a launcher like minimalist phone?

No. Curbox does not replace your home screen. It is an app blocker and screen time tool, so it works with whatever launcher you use, including minimalist phone.

Can I use minimalist phone and Curbox together?

Yes. A common split is to use the launcher for a calm home screen and Curbox for limits and unlock challenges. If both have blocking turned on, give them different apps so you do not get two pause screens in a row.

Is minimalist phone free?

Its Play listing offers monthly, annual, or one-time purchase plans, each with a free trial. Curbox is free with no paid tier.

Is there an open source minimal launcher?

Yes. Olauncher is a minimal launcher licensed under GPL v3 and available on F-Droid. It does not block apps, so pair it with a blocker such as Curbox if you need limits.

Do I need a computer for per-app grayscale?

minimalist phone's listing says its monochrome mode for selected apps needs activation on a PC or Mac. Curbox's Grayscale Filter needs the free Shizuku app, which on Android 11 and newer can be started with Wireless Debugging, so no computer is required.
