Alternatives · ScreenZen

Switching from ScreenZen? Here is the ledger.

ScreenZen is one of the few screen time apps that is free and supported by donations instead of a subscription. If you are looking at alternatives anyway, it is usually for a specific reason: you want code you can read, an app that never goes online, or more control over the moment you hit a limit. This page lays out honestly what changes if you move to Curbox on Android.

ScreenZen in a paragraph

ScreenZen runs on iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows. You choose apps and websites to block, then limit or fully block them with schedules or daily limits. Its best-known idea is a mindful pause before a distracting app opens. It has a lock mode that its site says "protects against common loopholes" and can prevent uninstalling. Its privacy policy says core blocking settings and data stay on your device, it uses no advertising networks, and an account (Apple ID or Google sign-in) is only needed for its optional social accountability and halo features. On Android it uses Accessibility to block apps, websites, Shorts, and Reels.

That is a solid app. The question is whether its trade-offs suit you.

The short version: if you use ScreenZen on more than one kind of device, or rely on friends for accountability, stay. If you are on Android and want an app you can audit, with more ways to make unlocking hard, Curbox is worth a week.

When ScreenZen is the better pick

  • You have an iPhone, or you want the same limits on a Mac or Windows PC. ScreenZen covers all of them. Curbox does not run on iOS.
  • Accountability works for you. Sharing screen time with friends is a ScreenZen feature with no Curbox equivalent.
  • You use a browser Curbox does not support and need site-level blocking inside it.
  • It already works. If your screen time is where you want it, switching costs more than it gives.

FAQ

Is Curbox free like ScreenZen?

Yes. Curbox is free with no subscription and no paid tier. It is open source under the GPL v3 and supported by donations.

Does Curbox have a pause before opening apps?

Yes. Put the app in an App Pause group with the On Each Open mode, and Curbox shows its pause screen on every launch. You can add a vibrating countdown, a wait, a challenge, or a personal message.

Can I use Curbox on an iPhone?

Not yet. Curbox runs on Android, with a browser extension for Chrome and Firefox. If you need iOS, ScreenZen is the better choice today.

Can I run Curbox and ScreenZen together?

Technically yes, but two apps blocking the same app will show two pause screens and fight over it. Test Curbox on one app first, then move the rest over.

Is Curbox more private than ScreenZen?

Both keep blocking data on your phone and use no ad networks. The Curbox Android app goes further: it has no internet permission at all, no analytics, and its full source code is public, so you can verify this yourself.

Does Curbox block Shorts and Reels like ScreenZen?

Yes. It blocks Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook Reels after a time limit or a video count, combined across all three. It can also show a live counter and your average attention span.
