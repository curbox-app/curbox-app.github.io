A StayFree alternative that keeps your usage data at home.

StayFree is one of the most installed screen time trackers on Android, and it does the job well. People look for a StayFree alternative for one reason more than any other: they want to know where the record of every app and site they use ends up. This page shows that path for both apps, then helps you rebuild your StayFree setup in Curbox.

What StayFree does well

Credit first. StayFree tracks and limits screen time on Android, iPhone and iPad, Windows, Mac and in the browser, and shows it in one dashboard with what its site calls "cross-device stats". It can block right now, block on a schedule, cap daily use for apps, sites or whole categories, cap a single session, and hide distracting parts of social media without blocking the whole site. It is free to download.

If you want one tracker across a laptop, a work PC and an iPhone, StayFree reaches further than Curbox does today. That is worth knowing before you switch.

Where does your StayFree data go?

A screen time tracker sees every app you open and, in supported browsers, every site. So the useful question is not whether it collects data (it has to, to work) but where that data travels afterwards. Here is the path each app describes in its own documents.

StayFree

Your phoneUsage access permission records app and website use.
You are asked for consentThe policy says the app asks before sending Collected Data.
Sensor Tower serversServices Data: app usage, website usage, sites visited, ad context and interactions, commerce information. Pseudonymized with a random Generic ID.
Anonymized insights productsThe policy says de-identified, aggregated information may be sold, and used to train models and for research.

Source: Sensor Tower Panel Privacy Policy, effective 18 November 2026, which names StayFree and ActionDash among the apps it covers.

Curbox

Your phoneThe App Blocker accessibility service records which app is on screen and, in supported browsers, the site.
Stays on your phoneThe Android app has no internet permission in any variant, so there is no second hop. Uninstalling deletes it.

Source: the Curbox privacy policy and the public GPL v3 source code, which anyone can check.

To be fair to StayFree: the policy asks for consent, says it will never sell personal information to advertising platforms or data brokers, and describes the data as pseudonymized. Whether that is acceptable is your call. Sensor Tower is a mobile market intelligence company, and the policy is open about the data feeding its products. If you would rather your app list never leaves the phone at all, a tracker without network access settles the question.

Rebuild your StayFree setup in Curbox

Most StayFree setups come down to a few limits and a schedule. This takes about five minutes after you install Curbox and grant the three onboarding permissions.

  1. Daily limits on your worst appsCreate an App Pause group in Usage Based mode with a minute cap. Add every app you limited in StayFree. Turn on auto-add if you want new installs covered too.Reducers → Apps → + → Usage Based
  2. Scheduled blocksA second group in Time Based mode allows apps only inside the hours you pick. One schedule cannot cross midnight, so split 11 PM to 7 AM into two blocks.Reducers → Apps → + → Time Based → Configure Allowed Schedule
  3. Session limitsCurbox does not cap a single session directly. The closest match is a Mindful Message with {live_session_duration} so you see how long you have been in the app, or Sip & Stretch, which enforces a break after a stretch of use.Reducers → Gentle Prompts
  4. Block nowFocus Mode is the one-tap version. Pick a Focus Group, set the timer, start.Focus tab → slider → choose group → confirm
  5. Website limitsAdd sites to a Keywords And Websites group. You can block one part of a site, such as youtube.com/shorts, and leave the rest.Reducers → Keywords And Websites → +
  6. Decide how hard the stop isStayFree mostly reminds and blocks. Curbox lets you choose what the pause screen asks for: nothing, a wait, a typed sentence, a QR code in another room, an NFC tag, or adaptive math.Any group → Configure Warning Screen

Want only the tracking? Skip all of the above, pin the Screentime widget, and look at the Usage tab guide.

When StayFree is the better pick

You track a computer too

StayFree has Windows and Mac apps. Curbox has a browser extension, not a desktop app.

You use an iPhone

StayFree is on iPhone and iPad. Curbox is not on iOS yet.

You want category limits

StayFree can cap a whole category of apps. In Curbox you build groups yourself.

The policy is fine by you

If you have read Sensor Tower's policy and are comfortable, StayFree's reach across devices is a real advantage.

Note that the UI Hider, which hides feeds inside apps, and anti-uninstall protection are in the F-Droid variant of Curbox and not the Playstore variant. Every official build is on the downloads page.

StayFree alternative FAQ

Is Curbox a good StayFree alternative?

If you use StayFree mainly on Android to see your screen time and cap a few apps, yes. Curbox covers per-app usage, website time in supported browsers, daily limits, schedules and blocking, and adds Reels and Shorts limits and unlock challenges. It does not cover Windows, Mac or iPhone, which StayFree does.

Does StayFree send my usage data anywhere?

StayFree is made by ST Pulse, a Sensor Tower company. Sensor Tower's Panel Privacy Policy, effective 18 November 2026, names StayFree and says the app asks for consent before sending Collected Data, including app usage and website usage, to Sensor Tower's servers. The policy says this data is pseudonymized and that de-identified, aggregated insights may be sold. Read the policy yourself for the full detail.

Does Curbox send my usage data anywhere?

No. The Curbox Android app has no internet permission in any variant, so it cannot send anything. Your usage history stays on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall.

Can I export my screen time from Curbox like I could in StayFree?

Yes. In the Usage tab, open the three-dot menu and choose Export. You can export a Daily Breakdown, a Total Summary, or both, as CSV.

Does Curbox block apps by category like StayFree?

Curbox limits apps in groups that you build yourself rather than by store category. An App Pause group can be set to pick up apps you install later, which covers the most common reason for category limits.

Is there a Curbox desktop app?

Not a desktop app. There is a Curbox browser extension for Chrome and Firefox that blocks websites with schedules and limits, tracks website time and runs focus mode.
