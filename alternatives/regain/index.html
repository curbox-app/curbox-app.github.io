Alternatives · Regain

A Regain app alternative with every lock included.

Regain, now sold as "Regain: Study Timer for Focus", is popular with students for a reason: it blocks Reels and Shorts without blocking the whole app, and its Strict Mode means you cannot quit early. If you want a Regain app alternative, the usual triggers are the Pro subscription, the account, or wanting a blocker that does not talk to a server. This page compares the two honestly and sets up a study session in Curbox.

What Regain gets right

Regain is built by EPowerX Labs in Bengaluru and runs on Android and iPhone. Its site lists a Strict Mode where "you can't quit early or uninstall the app until your focus time ends", a YouTube Study Mode that blocks Shorts and the home feed while allowing study channels you choose, Reels and Shorts blocking that leaves the rest of the app working, website blocking including adult content, 25-minute Pomodoro rounds, focus music, and Focus Groups with leaderboards for friends or exam peers.

That is a well-chosen set of features for students. The leaderboard in particular is something Curbox does not try to do.

Set up tonight's study session in Curbox

  1. Install Curbox and grant overlay, notifications and the App Blocker accessibility service.
  2. On the Focus tab, tap Create Focus Group and name it "Study".
  3. Pick Block All Except Selected and choose only what you need: calculator, notes, dictionary, music. See allowlist mode.
  4. Leave "Let me quit mid-sessions" off. Turn on Do Not Disturb.
  5. Turn on the Pomodoro timer: 25 minutes, 4 rounds, 5-minute breaks.
  6. Save, set the slider and start.

For more recipes, including a QR code you keep in another room, see the app blocker for studying guide.

When Regain is the better pick

  • You study better with friends watching. Regain's leaderboards and Focus Groups have no Curbox equivalent.
  • You need specific YouTube channels and nothing else. Regain's Study Mode allows chosen channels.
  • You are on an iPhone. Regain has an iOS app; Curbox does not yet.
  • You like an all-in-one study app with music and themes, and the Pro price is fine for you.

On the other side: Regain's privacy policy, last updated 5 November 2025, lists names, email addresses and device IDs among the data it collects, names Google Analytics, and lists ad networks and analytics services among the third parties it shares with. Its terms say users must be 18 or older. Curbox has no account, no analytics and no internet permission, and the source is public. Read both and decide.

Regain alternative FAQ

What is a good Regain app alternative on Android?

Curbox covers the core of what students use Regain for: blocking Reels and Shorts without blocking the whole app, Pomodoro focus sessions, sessions you cannot quit early, and website blocking. It is free with no paid tier, no account and no internet access. It does not have Regain's friend leaderboards, study music or iPhone app.

How much does Regain Pro cost?

Regain's refund policy, last updated 4 December 2025, lists Regain Pro on Android at INR 199 a month and INR 999 a year, with offers that can bring those lower. The core app is free; Pro adds premium themes and extra strict-blocking options, according to Regain's FAQ.

Can Curbox stop me quitting a focus session early?

Yes. When you create a Focus Group, leave "Let me quit mid-sessions" turned off. A session set up that way also cannot be ended early with an NFC tag. For extra protection against uninstalling, the F-Droid variant has anti-uninstall protection, and you can add Curbox itself to a blocking group.

Can Curbox allow only study channels on YouTube?

Not by channel. Curbox can block YouTube Shorts, block the youtube.com/shorts section in browsers, and in the F-Droid variant hide the YouTube home feed so only search results show. Searching for the lecture you need still works.

Does Curbox need an account like Regain?

No. Curbox has no accounts at all. Regain's terms say you may be required to register to use its services.

Does Curbox block adult websites?

Curbox blocks any site you add to a Keywords And Websites group, and you can import a ready-made list from a .txt file. It does not ship a built-in adult content filter.
