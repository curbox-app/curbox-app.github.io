Alternatives · Lock Me Out

Lock Me Out, translated into Curbox.

Lock Me Out has its own vocabulary: lockouts, breaks, prohibited changes, emergency access. If you are moving to Curbox, the hardest part is knowing what each of those is called now. This page is a dictionary. Look up the Lock Me Out term, see the Curbox equivalent, and how close the match is.

First, is switching worth it?

Lock Me Out is a solid app and we will not pretend otherwise. Its site promises "No ads!" and its privacy policy says it "does not collect or share any information" and keeps usage records on the device. If you are happy with it, there is no privacy emergency here.

People usually look for an alternative for one of three reasons:

  • The premium wall. Lock Me Out's premium version unlocks uninstall and tamper protection, an unlimited number of lockouts, location blocking and hiding notifications from blocked apps. It is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time payment. In Curbox, anti-uninstall and unlimited groups are free.
  • Blocking all of Instagram or YouTube is too blunt. Lock Me Out blocks apps and websites. Curbox can block only Reels and Shorts, or hide a feed, and leave the rest of the app working.
  • Trust you can check. Lock Me Out can read on-screen text to detect blocked websites and tampering, as any blocker of this kind must. Its source is not published. Curbox's full source is public under the GPL v3, and its Android app has no internet permission.

Three classic lockouts, rebuilt

The bedtime lock

Was: a repeating regular lockout, 11 PM to 7 AM.

  1. Reducers → Apps → +, select your night apps.
  2. Time Based, allowed 7 AM to 11 PM.
  3. Warning screen: Never Unlock.
  4. Optional: Auto DND from 11 PM to 12 AM and 12 AM to 7 AM.

The one-hour social cap

Was: a usage-based lockout on time in apps.

  1. Reducers → Apps → +, select the social apps.
  2. Usage Based, 60 minutes.
  3. Warning screen: Require effort, then QR or Barcode Scan with a code in another room.

The exam week

Was: allowed-apps mode with a password held by a friend.

  1. Focus → Create Focus Group, select study apps.
  2. Block All Except Selected, quitting off.
  3. Turn on Pomodoro with short breaks.
  4. F-Droid or GitHub build: turn on anti-uninstall.

When Lock Me Out is the better pick

Keep Lock Me Out if

You lock by number of device unlocks or total screen time, you depend on the lock-screen-only mode, you want location rules without a second app, or you like paying to end a lockout as a deterrent. It also lists support for split screen, picture-in-picture and Samsung pop-up views.

Move to Curbox if

You want tamper protection without paying, you want to limit Reels and Shorts rather than whole apps, you like unlock tasks better than passwords, or you want to read the code of the app that watches your screen.

Questions

Is there a free alternative to Lock Me Out premium?

Curbox is free and open source with no premium tier. Anti-uninstall protection, unlimited groups and every unlock challenge cost nothing. Location blocking is the exception: Curbox only offers it through a separate add-on app, and hiding notifications from blocked apps is not available.

Can Curbox lock my whole phone except the lock screen like Lock Me Out?

Not exactly. The closest setup is a Focus Group with Block All Except Selected, leaving only the apps you choose, such as Phone and Messages. Curbox does not have a lock-screen-only mode.

Can Curbox lock me out after a number of app opens or device unlocks?

Not directly. Curbox has no device-unlock counter. For app opens, the On Each Open mode shows the pause screen on every launch, and Proceed Limits cap how many times you can get past it in a time window, which gives a similar result.

Does Curbox have a password to end a lockout early?

No. Curbox replaces passwords with unlock challenges you choose in advance: Never Unlock, a wait, or an effort task such as scanning a QR code kept in another room. A Focus session can also be made impossible to quit.

Does Curbox have tamper and uninstall protection?

Yes, in the F-Droid and GitHub builds. Anti-uninstall uses device administrator access. The Playstore build does not include it. You can also add Curbox to one of its own app blocking groups so its settings are harder to reach on impulse.

Is Curbox more private than Lock Me Out?

Both say they keep usage data on the device. The difference is that you can verify Curbox: its full source is public under the GPL v3 and the Android app has no internet permission. Lock Me Out does not publish its source.
