Alternatives · Unpluq

An Unpluq alternative where any NFC tag or barcode is the key.

Unpluq is built around a nice idea: your distracting apps stay locked until you tap a physical tag against your phone. Leave the tag in another room and every unlock becomes a short walk. If you want that idea as an Unpluq alternative without buying a specific tag or paying a subscription, Curbox can turn a tag you already own, or a barcode on any box in your kitchen, into the key.

How Unpluq works

Unpluq sells the Unpluq Tag, a keychain-sized tag that uses NFC, together with the Unpluq app. You choose which apps to block, set custom schedules, and track screen time. To open a blocked app, you need "your physical Unpluq Tag (or one of the digital barriers)". The site puts it simply: "Your Tag is your key."

The tag needs a Premium subscription, which Unpluq says is "required for use". Its shop sells subscriptions of 3, 6 or 12 months, with or without a tag, and offers a 30-day money-back guarantee. It works on iPhones running iOS 16 or later and on Android. Unpluq's privacy policy says you need to create an account.

Why a physical key works at all

Most phone habits run on autopilot. The thumb finds the icon before you have decided anything. A key that lives somewhere else breaks that loop, because you have to stand up and go and get it. By the time you are holding it, you have had twenty or thirty seconds to notice what you were about to do. Some people go back to the sofa without the key.

Set up a tag-unlock in Curbox

Install Curbox from the downloads page and finish onboarding. Then:

  1. Pick the apps

    Go to Reducers → Apps, tap +, name the group and select the apps the key should guard. Choose On Each Open if you want the key every time, or Time Based to need it only outside certain hours.

  2. Choose the key

    Tap Configure Warning Screen, set the challenge to Require effort to unlock, and pick NFC Tag or QR or Barcode Scan. Register your tag or scan your code.

  3. Put the key somewhere awkward

    Another room works better than a drawer next to the sofa. A shelf by the front door, a kitchen cupboard or your bag in the hall are common choices.

  4. Optional: add a pause first

    A short delay timer before the challenge appears makes the phone vibrate and the screen brighten, which is often enough on its own.

  5. Optional: a focus tag

    Write a Focus Mode action to a second tag and stick it on your desk. Tap to start a session when you sit down.

More detail on tags, including what kind to buy, is on the NFC app blocker page. For the barcode route, see QR code unlock.

When Unpluq is the better pick

  • You use an iPhone, or want the same setup on iPhone and Android.
  • You want a finished product that arrives in a box, ready to go, with a money-back guarantee.
  • You would rather pay than spend ten minutes configuring groups and challenges.
  • You like Unpluq's digital barriers and the way its app is designed.

Curbox fits better if you want no subscription and no account, if you already have an NFC tag or would rather use a barcode, or if you also want limits for Reels and Shorts, focus sessions and usage stats in the same free app.

Questions people ask

Is there an Unpluq alternative without a subscription?

Yes. Curbox is free and open source. Its NFC Tag unlock challenge works with a writable NFC tag you register yourself, and its QR or Barcode Scan challenge can use a code you already own, such as the barcode on a product box. There is no subscription and no account.

Can I use my own NFC tag with Curbox?

Yes. You register a physical NFC tag in Curbox and tapping it against the phone becomes the way past the pause screen. Writable NFC stickers and key fobs are widely sold. Check that your phone has NFC turned on.

What if my phone does not have NFC?

Use the QR or Barcode Scan challenge instead. It uses the camera, so any code you can scan works, and you can place it in another room to add the same kind of physical distance.

Can an NFC tag start a focus session in Curbox?

Yes. Besides unlocking, Curbox can write a Focus Mode action to a writable tag: start focus, stop focus, or toggle it, with a Focus Group and duration you choose. A session marked as not quittable cannot be ended early with a tag.

Does Unpluq work on iPhone?

Yes. Unpluq lists compatibility with iPhones on iOS 16 or later and with Android devices. Curbox is Android only, plus a browser extension for Chrome and Firefox.

Does Curbox need an account like Unpluq?

No. Unpluq's privacy policy says users are required to create an account. Curbox has no accounts, and the Android app has no internet permission in any variant.
