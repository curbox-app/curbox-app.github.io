An ActionDash alternative for when watching is not enough.

ActionDash made Android's screen time dashboard available on every phone, and it is still a clean way to see your app usage, notifications and unlocks. People usually go looking for an ActionDash alternative after a few weeks of looking at the numbers and realising the numbers are not changing. Here is a metric-by-metric look at what carries over to Curbox, and what Curbox does with those numbers.

What ActionDash is today

ActionDash launched in 2019 from the developer of Action Launcher, as a way to get Google's Digital Wellbeing dashboard on phones that did not have it. Its site now describes four parts: screen time tracking with "app usage, notification counts, and unlock frequency", a Focus Mode that blocks apps on custom schedules, daily app usage limits that notify you as you get close, and usage insights over days, weeks and months. It runs on Android only.

Two newer things are worth knowing. The site now also sells ActionDash as an email manager that connects to your Gmail account for inbox insights and cleanup, with email data processed on its servers. And Sensor Tower's Panel Privacy Policy, effective 18 November 2026, names ActionDash among the apps it covers, along with StayFree and others.

Moving your ActionDash setup over

Install Curbox, grant the three onboarding permissions (overlay, notifications, and the App Blocker accessibility service), then:

  • App usage limits. Recreate each daily limit as an App Pause group in Usage Based mode. You can give weekdays and weekends different minutes in the same group. Reducers → Apps → + → Usage Based
  • Focus Mode schedules. For blocks that run every day, use a Time Based App Pause group. For blocks you start by hand, make a Focus Group and start it from the Focus tab. Focus tab → Create Focus Group
  • The dashboard habit. Pin the Screentime widget so the number is on your home screen, not inside an app you have to remember to open. Usage tab → ⋮ → Pin Widget
  • Notification overload. Curbox will not count notifications, but Scheduled Notifications can hold them and deliver them at a few times you pick. Reducers → Scheduled Notifications
  • Noisy stats. Hide background apps that inflate your total. Usage tab → ⋮ → Ignore List

When ActionDash is the better pick

Stay with ActionDash if:

  • Unlock counts and notification counts are the numbers you actually watch
  • You want the Gmail cleanup tools in the same app
  • You only want to see your usage, and a reminder near your limit is all the push you need
  • You have read the privacy policy and are comfortable with it

One more option: if all you want is the dashboard, your phone may already have one. See the Digital Wellbeing comparison.

ActionDash alternative FAQ

What is a good ActionDash alternative?

If you liked ActionDash's usage dashboard but want limits that actually stop you, Curbox is a free, open source option for Android. It tracks per-app and website time, then lets you block apps on a schedule or after a daily cap, with an unlock challenge you choose. If you only want a dashboard, Android's built-in Digital Wellbeing may be enough.

Does Curbox count unlocks and notifications like ActionDash?

No. Curbox documents screen time per app, website time, a usage heatmap, a short video count and attention span, but not unlock counts or notification counts. If those two numbers matter most to you, ActionDash shows them.

Who makes ActionDash now?

ActionDash was launched in 2019 by the developer of Action Launcher. Sensor Tower's Panel Privacy Policy, effective 18 November 2026, now lists ActionDash and actiondash.com among the apps and services it covers.

Does ActionDash read my email?

Only if you use its optional Email Manager. ActionDash's site says that feature integrates with your Gmail account and that email data processed on its servers is encrypted. The usage dashboard works without it.

How do I move my ActionDash limits to Curbox?

Create an App Pause group in Usage Based mode, add the apps you limited in ActionDash, and set the same daily minutes. Then choose a warning screen so the limit is a stop rather than a reminder.

Is Curbox free?

Yes. Curbox is free and open source under GPL v3, with no ads, no account and no paid tier.
