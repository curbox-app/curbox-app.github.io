A BlockSite alternative that keeps your browsing on your phone.

BlockSite is one of the most installed site blockers, on Android, iPhone and several desktop browsers. People start looking for a BlockSite alternative for two reasons: the free plan feels tight, or they read the privacy policy. This page walks through both, then shows how to move your blocked sites, schedules and focus sessions into Curbox on Android.

What a site blocker can see, and where it goes

Any Android app that blocks websites inside other browsers needs the Accessibility Service, which can read what is on your screen, including the address bar. That is true of BlockSite and of Curbox. The difference is what each app does with that view.

BlockSite's privacy policy (last modified 16 July 2026) says it collects browsing data and activity data, such as which apps you use, and that on Android some of this is gathered through the Accessibility Service. It lists third parties including Google Analytics, Firebase, Hotjar, Taboola, the Facebook Pixel and Google AdMob, and says affiliates may use personal information for market intelligence or analytics. The policy also says BlockSite does not sell data for money.

BlockSite features and their Curbox swaps

Most of what you use in BlockSite has a direct counterpart. A few things do not, and those are marked.

  • Block sites and appsBlockSite block list → Reducers → Keywords And Websites for sites, Reducers → Apps for apps. You can block a whole site or only a section, such as reddit.com/r/all.
  • Schedulesdaily or weekly → App Pause groups in Time Based mode, with Configure Allowed Schedule for hours and days. For a browser-wide schedule, add the browser itself to the group.
  • Usage Limitdaily screen time goal → Usage Based App Pause groups: a daily allowance in minutes, with optional different limits per weekday.
  • Focus Modetimed work sessions → Focus Mode with Focus Groups of apps and websites, an optional Pomodoro timer and Focus Stats.
  • Custom block pageyour own image and text → Custom Message on the warning screen, or a random message from your list. No custom images.
  • Password protectionlock your settings → Different approach
    No password. Curbox uses Never Unlock, Proceed Limits and effort challenges, and the F-Droid variant adds anti-uninstall. You can also add Curbox itself to an App Pause group.
  • Category blockingwhole categories of sites → Not built in
    Curbox has no ready-made categories. You can import a .txt keyword list, one entry per line, and the community Discord shares lists.
  • Accountability partnerfriend notified on bypass → Not available
    Curbox has no way to alert another person. It cannot reach the internet to send one.
  • Sync across deviceswith a BlockSite account → The Curbox browser extension can optionally pair with the Curbox Sync companion app on your phone. No account, no Curbox server.

Rewriting your block list for Curbox

Curbox matches what you type against the address bar, so the list you had in BlockSite usually carries over as-is. These forms let you be more precise than a plain domain.

You do not need https:// or www.. Website blocking works in Chrome, Brave, Firefox, Opera, Cromite, Vanadium, Waterfox and several other listed browsers. For any other browser, block the browser app with App Pause. You can try patterns before setting them up with the tester on the website blocking guide.

Switching in ten minutes

  1. Before uninstalling BlockSite, copy your blocked sites into a note, one per line.
  2. Install Curbox from the downloads page and grant Screen Overlay, Notifications and the App Blocker accessibility service in onboarding.
  3. Save the note as a .txt file, open Reducers → Keywords And Websites, create a group, and use the three-dot menu → Import. Blank lines and duplicates are skipped.
  4. Open Configure Warning Screen and choose what happens on a blocked page: Never Unlock, a wait, or a task.
  5. Recreate schedules and daily limits as App Pause groups, then remove BlockSite so two blockers are not fighting over the same screen.

When BlockSite is the better pick

  • You want the same blocker on an iPhone, Safari or Edge as well as Android.
  • An accountability partner who gets told when you bypass a block matters to you.
  • You want ready-made category blocking without building a list.
  • You prefer a password lock to an unlock challenge.

BlockSite alternative FAQ

What is a good BlockSite alternative for Android?

If privacy is your reason for leaving, look for a blocker with no internet permission and public source code. Curbox is one: it blocks sites in many Android browsers, blocks apps and Reels or Shorts, and keeps everything on the phone.

Does BlockSite collect my browsing history?

Its privacy policy says it collects browsing data and activity data, partly through the Android Accessibility Service, and lists several third-party services. Read the current policy on blocksite.co for the exact wording.

Is Curbox free, or does it have a premium plan like BlockSite?

Curbox is free with no premium plan. Every feature is available to everyone, and the project is funded by donations.

Can Curbox password-protect my settings?

No. Instead it uses unlock challenges: Never Unlock, a wait, or a task such as scanning a QR code. The F-Droid variant can also resist being uninstalled.

Which browsers does Curbox block websites in?

Chrome, Brave, Firefox, Fennec, Opera, Cromite, Vanadium, Helium, CalyxOS Chromium, the /e/OS browser and Waterfox. For any other browser, block the browser app itself with App Pause.

Is there a Curbox extension for desktop like BlockSite?

Yes. The Curbox browser extension for Chrome and Firefox blocks websites with schedules and limits, filters Reels and Shorts feeds and runs focus mode.
