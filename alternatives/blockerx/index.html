Alternatives · BlockerX

Looking for a BlockerX alternative? Check the fit first.

BlockerX is built mainly to block adult content, with accountability partners and community support on top. Curbox is a general app and website blocker. They overlap, but they are not the same kind of tool, and picking the wrong one for this particular goal can make things harder. This page is an honest look at when Curbox is a good BlockerX alternative and when it is not.

What BlockerX is built for

BlockerX describes itself on its homepage as a porn blocker that can "block over 2 million adult sites". Around that core it lists keyword restrictions, safe search enforcement, a custom block list, image blocking, social media, gaming and dating app blockers, stats, articles and video courses, an accountability partner feature, alerts, a community, and uninstall protection on Android. It has apps for Android and iOS, a Chrome extension, and Windows and macOS applications.

On its premium page when we checked, BlockerX offered a 12-month plan and a lifetime plan, shown as discounted offers, with a one-day free trial on the yearly plan. It lists "No ads on BlockerX App" as a premium benefit, and notes that the premium features apply to the mobile apps only.

What Curbox is, and what it is not

Before anything else, here is the plain version.

Curbox is

  • An app blocker with daily limits, schedules and focus sessions
  • A website blocker that reads the address bar in supported browsers and matches your own keywords, domains, paths and regex
  • A set of pause screens you can make very hard to get past
  • Offline, account-free and open source

Curbox is not

  • A curated adult content blocklist
  • An image or safe search filter
  • A network, VPN or DNS filter that covers every app and browser
  • An accountability service that sends reports to another person

Four questions to decide if Curbox fits

  1. Do you need a list of sites someone else maintains?
    YesA dedicated filter like BlockerX does this. Curbox only blocks what you add, so you would be building the list yourself. NoIf a handful of keywords and patterns cover your triggers, Curbox's keyword syntax is flexible enough, especially with regex.
  2. Does another person need to see reports?
    YesStay with an accountability tool. Curbox has no accounts and cannot send anything off your phone. NoThen the lack of a server is an advantage: nothing about your browsing leaves the device.
  3. Is the hard part the moment of the urge?
    YesThis is where Curbox is strongest: delay timers, Never Unlock, Proceed Limits, and Urge Surfing to ride a craving out instead of unlocking. NoIf you mainly want content kept out of reach in the background, a filter matters more than the pause screen.
  4. Do you need it on an iPhone or computer too?
    YesBlockerX covers more platforms. Curbox is Android plus a browser extension for Chrome and Firefox. NoOn Android alone, Curbox can cover browsers and apps with one set of rules.

Curbox

If you switch: a Curbox setup that holds

This setup leans on friction and on removing escape routes, since that is what Curbox does best. Install Curbox from the downloads page first.

  1. Go to Reducers → Keywords And Websites, tap +, and add the words and domains you want blocked. Plain words match a domain segment, so one word can cover many regional sites. For several related words, one r: regex keeps the list short. Test patterns first: a broken regex silently does nothing.
  2. Tap Configure Warning Screen and choose Never Unlock for this group, so there is no proceed button at all.
  3. Close the gaps. Keyword blocking only works in the listed browsers, so put any other browser you have installed into an App Pause group with On Each Open and Never Unlock.
  4. Set the App Pause group to automatically add apps you install later, so a new browser does not slip through.
  5. Add Curbox itself to an App Pause group so its settings cannot be changed on impulse. If you use the F-Droid variant, turn on anti-uninstall protection as well.
  6. Turn on Urge Surfing so the block screen offers an "I'm struggling" button for the moments that are hardest.

If you are working on a compulsive habit that feels out of your control, a blocker is one tool among several. Talking to someone you trust, or to a professional, can make a bigger difference than any setting on this page.

When BlockerX is the better pick

  • You want a large blocklist maintained for you instead of writing your own.
  • You want an accountability partner to receive reports or alerts.
  • You want image blocking or safe search enforcement.
  • You need the same protection on iOS, Windows or macOS.
  • You value the courses and community that come with it.

Curbox makes more sense if your main need is friction at the moment of the urge, if you want nothing leaving your phone, or if a free, open source tool matters to you. You can also use both: a filter for content and Curbox for habits.

Questions people ask

Is Curbox a porn blocker like BlockerX?

Not in the same way. BlockerX is built as an adult content blocker with a large ready-made blocklist and accountability features. Curbox is a general app and website blocker. It blocks the words, domains, paths and patterns you add yourself in supported browsers, and any app you choose, but it does not ship a curated adult-site list or filter images.

Can Curbox block websites by keyword?

Yes. Under Reducers, Keywords And Websites, you can add domains, paths, single words that match a domain, wildcards, and regular expressions starting with r:. It works in Chrome, Brave, Firefox, Opera and several other listed browsers. Browsers that are not supported can be blocked as whole apps with App Pause.

Does Curbox have an accountability partner feature?

No. Curbox has no accounts and the Android app has no internet permission, so it cannot send reports to another person. If accountability matters to you, BlockerX or a separate arrangement with someone you trust may suit you better.

Can Curbox stop me from uninstalling it?

The F-Droid variant has anti-uninstall protection that uses device administrator access. The Playstore variant does not include it. You can also add Curbox itself to an App Pause group and use Never Unlock, so its settings are harder to change in a weak moment.

Is Curbox free?

Yes. Curbox is free and open source under the GPL v3. There is no premium tier and no ads.

Can I use Curbox and a content filter together?

Yes. Curbox works at the app and address-bar level, so it can sit alongside a network or DNS-level filter you already use. A filter for content and Curbox for habits and friction cover different gaps, so they can complement each other.
