What Clearspace does

Clearspace, from clearspace technologies, inc., is built around one idea: when you tap an app you chose, decide whether to go ahead after a deep breath or back out. Its website describes a short pause that shows your remaining uses for the day, and two ways to earn more time when you run out: Push-Up to Scroll, where a set of push-ups buys more minutes, and Breathe to Scroll, a guided breathing exercise. It also offers friend challenges with a leaderboard, focus schedules, daily streaks, screen time reporting, and accountability partners. Personal and Family are separate apps.

On Google Play it is listed as free with in-app purchases and more than 100,000 downloads. Its Data safety section says it may collect location, personal info, and other data types, and may share some of them with third parties. Data is encrypted in transit and you can request deletion.

When Clearspace is the better pick

Stay with Clearspace if you use an iPhone, if the push-ups are what make it work for you, or if friend challenges and accountability partners keep you going. Curbox deliberately has no accounts, which means no social layer at all. Clearspace also has a separate Family app; Curbox is built for managing your own phone.

Switch if you want the pause and the limits without paying, if you would rather your usage never leave the phone, or if you need finer control, such as blocking only Reels or Shorts, or hiding YouTube recommendations (UI Hider is in the F-Droid variant).

Clearspace alternative FAQ

Is Clearspace available on Android?

Yes. Clearspace is on Google Play from clearspace technologies, inc., as well as on iPhone. Personal and Family are separate apps.

Is Clearspace free?

It is free to download, with premium features sold as in-app purchases. Curbox is free with no paid tier and no subscription.

Can Curbox make me do push-ups to unlock an app?

No. Curbox does not count exercise with the camera. It can make unlocking take effort in other ways: scanning a QR code or barcode, tapping an NFC tag, solving adaptive math, typing a sentence, or writing down your intent.

Does Curbox have a breathing pause like Clearspace?

It has the building blocks: a Wait to unlock screen, a vibrating delay timer before the screen appears, and a custom message such as "Breathe first." Together they recreate the pause, though there is no guided breathing animation.

Does Curbox need an account?

No. There are no accounts, and the Android app has no internet permission, so usage data stays on your phone.
