Alternatives · Forest

A Forest app alternative for when a tree is not enough.

Forest turned focus into something you can see: you plant a seed, it grows while you stay off your phone, and it dies if you leave. That works beautifully for some people. If you are looking for a Forest app alternative, it is often because the tree stopped mattering, or because your problem is not one study session but the scrolling in between.

What Forest is today

Forest runs on iPhone, iPad and Android, with browser extensions for Chrome and Firefox. Its core is free: the focus timer, Deep Focus Mode, which blocks apps that are not allowed, focus tracking and planting together with friends, where if anyone gives up the whole forest falls. The free tier shows occasional ads, never during an active session.

A paid subscription called Forest Plus adds a custom app allowlist, time limits on more than one app, focus analytics, pausing during a session, an ad-free experience, and the option to spend coins planting real trees with Trees for the Future. Forest says Plus pricing varies by region and platform, and the old one-time Pro upgrade is no longer sold to new users.

Neither approach is better for everyone. If you open Forest and genuinely care about the tree, keep it. If you have learned to let the tree die, a rule that makes you stand up and walk to another room is harder to ignore.

Build a Forest-style session in Curbox

If you liked how Forest sessions felt, this recreates the shape of one, minus the tree.

The 25-minute seedling
Focus Mode · about 3 minutes to set up
  1. Open the Focus tab and tap Create Focus Group. Name it "Study".
  2. Tap Select Apps and choose the few you need, such as a notes app, a dictionary and music.
  3. Set the block mode to Block All Except Selected, so every other app is off-limits.
  4. Leave Let me quit mid-sessions off. This is your "the tree dies" rule, except the session simply does not end.
  5. Optionally turn on Turn on do not disturb and the Pomodoro timer, for example four rounds with 5-minute breaks.
  6. Save the group, set the slider to 25 minutes, and confirm.
  7. Afterwards, tap the bar-chart icon by the slider to see the session in Focus Stats.

Want the session to start without opening the app? Write it to an NFC tag on your desk and tap your phone against it. And to make your evening scrolling cost something too, add a rule like "no Instagram until I have focused for an hour today" with the warning screen's Link to Focus Mode option.

The scrolling between sessions

Forest is at its best inside a session. The hard part for many people is the rest of the day: the five minutes before a lecture, the bus ride, the hour after dinner that turns into three. A focus timer does nothing there, because you never started one.

That is where Curbox's standing rules come in. A Short-Form Video Content limit stops Reels and Shorts after a number of videos you choose, counted across Instagram, YouTube and Facebook together. A Usage Based App Pause group gives a social app 30 minutes a day and no more. A Mindful Messages overlay can show how long you have been in an app right now. None of these need you to remember to press start.

When Forest is the better pick

  • A visible reward keeps you going, and you do care whether the tree lives.
  • You study with friends and like the shared forest.
  • Planting real trees is part of what motivates you.
  • You use an iPhone or iPad.

Forest app alternative FAQ

Is Forest free on Android?

Forest's core focus features are free, with occasional ads outside sessions. Forest Plus is a paid subscription whose price varies by region and platform.

What is a good Forest app alternative for Android?

If you want blocking that works all day, not only during sessions, Curbox is a free, open source option. It has focus sessions with an allowlist, plus daily app limits, Reels and Shorts limits and unlock challenges.

Does Curbox grow trees or give rewards?

No. Curbox has no trees, coins or badges. It tracks your focus sessions, completion rate and day streak, and relies on friction rather than rewards.

Can I stop myself from quitting a session early?

Yes. Leave "Let me quit mid-sessions" off in the Focus Group, and the session cannot be ended before the timer runs out.

Is there an allowlist mode like Forest's?

Yes, and it is free. Pick Block All Except Selected when you create a Focus Group, and only the apps you chose stay available during the session.

Does Curbox work with friends like Plant Together?

No. Curbox has no accounts or social features, and the Android app has no internet access, so it cannot connect to other people's phones.
