Alternatives · Social Fever

A Social Fever alternative that does more than keep count.

Social Fever, made by Systweak, is an Android app that tracks how long you spend on your phone and nudges you with alerts, reports and healthy-habit reminders. If you are looking for a Social Fever alternative, it is usually because counting was not enough. This page shows where Curbox goes further, what you would give up, and how to move your setup across.

What Social Fever does

Systweak describes Social Fever as an app to "break phone addiction by monitoring phone usage". Its product page lists real-time app usage tracking, a "quality time" profile to restrict phone use, alerts and weekly reports, warnings when you look at the screen or listen to music for a long time, a water intake tracker with reminders, and a place to add hobbies you want to spend more time on. It runs on Android 4.0 and up.

That is a gentle, awareness-first approach, and for some people it is exactly right. The product page talks about alerts and reports. It does not describe blocking an app outright, blocking only part of an app such as Reels, or making you do anything before you can carry on.

From noticing to stopping: where each app sits

Screen time tools sit somewhere on a scale. At one end they help you notice. At the other end they stop you. Most people who outgrow a tracker need something further along that scale, but not always all the way to a hard block.

Why people look for a Social Fever alternative

The pattern is familiar. A tracker shows you a number, the number is bigger than you hoped, and an alert pops up while you are already deep in a feed. You dismiss it. Awareness is a real first step, but alerts compete with the very app that is holding your attention, and the app usually wins.

What tends to help next is friction at the moment of the tap: a pause screen, a short wait, a code you have to walk to, or a daily cap that ends the session for you. That is the part Curbox is built around. You decide in a calm moment how hard getting past a limit should be, and the pause screen holds you to it later.

The other common reason is short-form video. If the hours are going to Reels or Shorts rather than to Instagram or YouTube as a whole, a per-app timer is too blunt. Curbox can limit Reels and Shorts by time or by number of videos, counted across Instagram, YouTube and Facebook together, while messages and the rest of each app keep working.

Moving your setup to Curbox

Plan on about fifteen minutes. Get the app from the downloads page first.

  1. Finish onboarding. Curbox asks for Screen Overlay, Notifications and the App Blocker accessibility service. Usage tracking starts as soon as the service is on.
  2. Spend a day or two only watching. Open the Usage tab and pin the Screentime Widget from the three-dot menu so the number is on your home screen.
  3. Replace your alert with a message. Go to Reducers → Gentle Prompts, turn on Mindful Messages, select your heaviest app and write something like {app_usage_today} in here today. Still worth it?
  4. When you are ready for a limit, go to Reducers → Apps, tap +, select the app and choose Usage Based with a daily allowance.
  5. Tap Configure Warning Screen. A short delay timer, or Type a Sentence, is a good middle ground between an alert and a hard lock.
  6. If you used Social Fever's water reminders, turn on Sip & Stretch under Reducers and pick a break length.

When Social Fever is the better pick

Stay with Social Fever if:

  • You want a light tracker with healthy-habit extras, such as logging water and listing hobbies, and you do not want anything blocked.
  • Alerts and a weekly report are already changing how you use your phone. If it works, there is no reason to add friction.
  • You like having health-style reminders, such as long-screen and long-listening warnings, in the same app as your usage numbers.

Questions people ask

Is there a free Social Fever alternative for Android?

Yes. Curbox is free and open source under the GPL v3, with no subscription and no paid tier. It tracks app usage like Social Fever does, and adds limits that actually block an app when you reach them.

Does Curbox have water intake reminders like Social Fever?

Not a water log. The closest feature is Sip & Stretch: after a set amount of use in the apps you choose, Curbox blocks apps for a short break so you can drink water and move. It enforces the break rather than logging glasses.

Can Curbox just track my usage without blocking anything?

Yes. You can install it, grant the permissions and only use the Usage tab, the Screentime widget and the Reels counter. Nothing is blocked until you set up a limit.

Does Curbox send my usage data anywhere?

No. The Android app has no internet permission in any variant, so your usage history stays on your phone. There is no account to create.

Can I keep my Social Fever history when I switch?

Not directly. Curbox starts counting from the day you install it. If you want a record of your old numbers, note them down or export what Social Fever offers before you uninstall it.
