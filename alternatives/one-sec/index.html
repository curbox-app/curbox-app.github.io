Alternatives · one sec

The pause before the app, without the one-app limit.

one sec made a simple idea popular: put a short, deliberate pause between you and a distracting app. It works, and there is published research behind it. People look for an alternative when they hit the free tier, which covers one app, or when they want the pause on Android with friction they can tune. This page walks through the moment you open Instagram in each app, then shows how to build the same pause in Curbox.

What one sec is

one sec inserts what it calls an interruption before a distracting app or website opens. Its FAQ lists a deep breath prompt, breathing techniques like 4-7-8 and box breathing, small tasks such as a math problem, "Rotate Your Phone" or "Follow the Dot", reminders of healthy alternatives, a mirror, and intention or emotion tracking. Interruptions can appear right away, after a set number of minutes, or repeat over time. It can also fully block apps during a session or schedule.

It runs on iPhone and iPad, Android (since 2023), Mac, and as a browser extension for Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. It has no ads. The company points to a 2023 study in PNAS, run with researchers from the Max Planck Institute and Heidelberg University, and says one sec reduces app openings by 57% on average. That is the company's summary; the study itself is linked in the sources below.

Build a one sec style pause in Curbox

This takes about five minutes after the onboarding permissions (Screen Overlay, Notifications, and the App Blocker accessibility service).

  1. Open Reducers › Apps and tap +. Name the group "Pause first".
  2. Tap Select Apps and choose every app you open on autopilot. Unlike a free one sec setup, you can add as many as you like.
  3. Choose On Each Open as the blocking mode, so the pause screen shows on every launch.
  4. Tap Configure Warning Screen. Add a delay timer of 10 to 15 seconds. Even a short countdown often breaks the automatic tap.
  5. Pick the friction. For the gentlest version, choose Wait to unlock with no extra task. For a reflective version, choose Require effort to unlock and State Your Intent.
  6. Add a Custom Message. Short questions work best: "What did you come here for?"
  7. Turn on the "I'm struggling" button for Urge Surfing, and set a Proceed Limit if you want a cap on how often you can continue.
  8. Tap Done. The group is active right away. Try opening one of the apps.

Tip: want a pause only after a while, not on every open? Use a Usage Based group instead, with a small daily allowance, and the same warning screen. For short video specifically, the Short-Form Video Content reducer can stop Reels and Shorts after a set number of videos.

When one sec is the better pick

  • You use an iPhone, iPad, or Mac. one sec runs there. Curbox does not run on iOS yet.
  • Guided breathing is what works for you. one sec has several breathing techniques built in. Curbox only has a countdown and a message.
  • You want one subscription for all your devices, including website interruptions in Safari or Edge.
  • Your employer or school provides it. one sec sells team licenses.

FAQ

Does Curbox have a breathing exercise like one sec?

Not a guided one. Curbox's delay timer gives you a countdown with a brighter screen and vibration before you can continue, and you can show a message like "Take one slow breath". For bad cravings, Urge Surfing offers a three minute puzzle or movement break.

Can Curbox add a pause to more than one app for free?

Yes. Curbox is free for every feature and every app. Put as many apps as you want in an On Each Open group and each one shows the pause screen when it launches.

Is there research showing that a pause before opening an app works?

Yes. A 2023 study published in PNAS by Grüning, Riedel, and Lorenz-Spreen tested the one sec app and found people opened target apps less often. one sec reports this as 57% fewer openings on average. The study tested one sec itself, not Curbox, but the mechanism, a short delay before a habitual action, is the same idea Curbox's delay timer uses.

Can Curbox block websites like the one sec browser extension?

On Android, Curbox blocks websites in Chrome, Brave, Firefox, Opera, and other supported browsers by matching the address bar. For desktop browsers there is a Curbox extension for Chrome and Firefox with website blocking, schedules, and focus mode.

Do I need an account to use Curbox?

No. There are no accounts in the Curbox Android app, and it has no internet permission. Everything stays on your phone.

Can I make the pause harder to skip than one sec's?

Yes, if you want to. Curbox lets you require a QR or barcode scan, an NFC tag, adaptive math, or a typed sentence, add a dynamic wait you cannot predict, cap how many times you can continue, or tie unlocks to time spent in Focus Mode.
