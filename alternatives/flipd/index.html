A Flipd alternative for studying on Android.

Flipd is a focus and study timer with a strong community side: live study rooms, lofi music, stats and milestones. People look for a Flipd alternative when they want stricter blocking during a session, or when the free tier's limits get in the way. Here is a session compared stage by stage, so you can see exactly what changes if you switch to Curbox.

What Flipd offers today

Flipd's website calls it the place "where the productivity community meets". It lists customizable task timers for solo or group studying, break tracking, live public and private study rooms, free background music and sounds, activity tags, historical stats, milestone badges, and friendly competition in groups. It runs on iOS and Android.

App blocking works differently on each platform. Flipd says that on Android "you can lock your distracting apps by turning on Focus Lock mode inside of the Flipd Android app", while on iOS hiding apps moved to a separate app called Focus Lock after a change in Apple's developer policies.

What you would miss from Flipd

Live study rooms

Studying alongside others, in public or private rooms. Curbox has nothing social.

Music and sounds

Flipd's lofi library. Curbox only plays short sounds when a session or break starts and ends.

Milestones and competition

Badges and friendly group competition. Curbox shows a day streak, nothing more.

iPhone support

Flipd runs on iOS. Curbox is Android only, plus a browser extension.

Activity tags

Flipd lets you tag sessions by subject. Curbox's closest idea is separate Focus Groups, which stats can filter by.

Manual sessions

Flipd Premium can log sessions after the fact. Curbox only counts sessions it ran.

Rebuild your Flipd routine in Curbox

Install Curbox from the downloads page, finish onboarding, then:

  1. Open the Focus tab and tap Create Focus Group. Name it after the subject or task, such as "Revision".
  2. Tap Select Apps and choose your usual distractions. If you would rather allow only a few apps, such as a dictionary and your notes, use Block All Except Selected instead.
  3. Optionally Add Websites, for example youtube.com, so the browser does not become the way around.
  4. Turn on Pomodoro timer, set your break minutes and number of rounds. 25 minutes of focus with 5-minute breaks is a common start.
  5. Turn on Turn on do not disturb and leave Let me quit mid-sessions off. Tap Save Group.
  6. Set the slider, choose the group and start. Check Focus Stats from the bar-chart icon at the end of the week.

For more session ideas, see the study setups guide and the Pomodoro page.

When Flipd is the better pick

  • Studying with others in live rooms is what keeps you at your desk.
  • You like built-in focus music and do not want a separate app for it.
  • You switch between an iPhone and an Android phone, or study with friends who use iPhones.
  • Badges, milestones and group competition motivate you more than a lock does.

Curbox is the better fit if the problem is your phone pulling you away mid-session, if you want daily limits outside of study time too, or if you want all of it for free.

Questions people ask

Is there a free Flipd alternative for Android?

Curbox is free and open source. Its Focus Mode blocks the apps and websites you choose for a timed session, with a Pomodoro option, Do Not Disturb, and stats on sessions, total focus time and your day streak. There is no paid tier.

Does Flipd still block apps on Android?

According to Flipd's website, Android users can lock distracting apps by turning on Focus Lock mode inside the Flipd Android app. On iOS that feature moved to a separate app called Focus Lock.

Does Curbox have study rooms or lofi music like Flipd?

No. Curbox has no social features and no music. It focuses on blocking, focus sessions and usage stats. If studying alongside others or the built-in music keeps you going, Flipd covers that and Curbox does not.

Can Curbox do Pomodoro timers?

Yes. In a Focus Group you can turn on the Pomodoro timer, which repeats your focus time for a number of rounds with a break of the length you choose in between. Each round counts as a session in Focus Stats, and Curbox plays a sound when a session or break starts and ends.

Can I stop myself from quitting a focus session early in Curbox?

Yes. Leave the Let me quit mid-sessions option off in your Focus Group and the session runs until the timer ends.
