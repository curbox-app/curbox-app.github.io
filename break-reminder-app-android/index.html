Feature · Sip & Stretch

A break reminder app you can't swipe away.

Most break reminder apps for Android send a notification telling you to take a break from your phone. You swipe it away and keep scrolling. Curbox does the opposite: when you have spent long enough in the apps you chose, it pauses them for a few minutes, so the break actually happens.

Why "remind me to take a break" rarely works

A reminder asks you to make a decision at the worst possible time. When you are thirty minutes into a scroll, the part of you that wanted a break is not the one holding the phone. The notification arrives, you mean to act on it, and a minute later it is gone.

An enforced break removes the decision. You chose the rule earlier, while you were calm, and the phone simply follows it. That is the same idea behind every tool in Curbox: decide in a calm moment, let the app hold the line in the impulsive one.

It also helps your body, not only your screen time. Long sessions bent over a phone usually mean no water, no movement and a stiff neck. Sip & Stretch is built around a short, physical break: drink some water, stand up, stretch.

How the break timer counts your phone time

Here is one evening with a rule of a 5 minute break after 30 minutes of use. The minutes are an example, not a recommendation.

12m 18m 5m 10m 6m
7:00 break reset
Using chosen appsAway from themEnforced break
  1. You scroll for 12 minutes, then put the phone down for 2. That short gap is not a full break, so the timer does not start over.
  2. Another 18 minutes brings you to 30. The break starts: your apps are blocked for 5 minutes.
  3. You get water and stretch. When the break ends, everything works again.
  4. Later you step away for 6 minutes. Because that is at least as long as one break, the timer starts over from zero. Time away from the chosen apps counts as rest.

That last rule matters. If you already took a real break on your own, Curbox does not force another one on you.

Set up Sip & Stretch

Install Curbox from the downloads page, then open Sip & Stretch in the Reducers tab. There are only two settings plus the list of apps it watches.

AppsThe apps whose use counts toward a break. Usually your feeds and video apps, not maps or messages.
Break afterHow much use of those apps triggers a break. Pick a number slightly shorter than your usual "how did an hour go by" session.
Break lengthHow long the break lasts. All apps are blocked until it ends. A few minutes is enough to drink water and stand up; long breaks tend to get switched off.

Picking your numbers

Look at your screen time stats first. If your longest sessions in a feed usually run 45 minutes, a break after 30 catches them before they turn into an hour, without interrupting a quick check. Start a little looser than you think you need. A rule you keep for a month does more than a strict one you turn off on day two, and you can always tighten it once breaks feel normal.

When a simple reminder app is the better choice

An enforced break is not for everyone. If you want a nudge to drink water every hour regardless of what you are doing, a plain reminder or your phone's clock app does that with no special permissions. Sip & Stretch only counts time in the apps you pick, and it needs Curbox's accessibility service to see which app is open.

It is also the wrong tool for work you cannot pause. Leave navigation, calls and anything you need on call off the list. The point is to interrupt scrolling, not your life.

Break reminder FAQ

Is there an app that makes me take a break from my phone?

Yes. Curbox's Sip & Stretch blocks all apps for a short break once you have used your chosen apps for a set time. The break ends on its own after the length you picked.

Can I dismiss the break?

Not like a notification. Apps stay blocked until the break ends. If you need your phone, keep the break short; a few minutes is enough.

Does time away from my phone count?

Yes. Time away from the chosen apps counts as rest. If you stay away for as long as one break, the timer starts over, so you are not forced into a break you already took.

How is this different from Pomodoro?

Pomodoro breaks are planned gaps between focus rounds that you start on purpose. Sip & Stretch kicks in when you have been scrolling too long without meaning to. Curbox has both.
